**Challenge Title:** Inadequate Drinking Water Access and Quality

**Description:**
Drinking water remains the most frequently reported concern across schools, with parents consistently reporting lack of mineral/filtered water, impure water supply, and insufficient water availability. Parents describe situations where "children are having a lot of trouble bathing, doing daily activities" and "no proper drinking water facility" exists. Common complaints include "water problem," "no mineral water facility," "drinking water is not purified," and children receiving "bore water" or "muddy water." Many schools lack functional RO plants despite urgent need.
