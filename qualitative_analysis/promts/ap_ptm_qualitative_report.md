## Metric 3. Challenges That Require Attention

---

**Challenge Title:** Drinking Water Access and Purification Crisis

**Description:**  
The most pervasive and urgent issue across the survey is the absence of safe, purified drinking water. Parents across 17 districts report schools and hostels lack functioning RO plants, provide bore water directly to children, or supply filtered water only once per day. In NANDYAL (Laxmapuram), at least 8 parents independently reported no water plant exists. In Y.S.R. (Chinnachowk and Kamalapuram), parents describe receiving no mineral water, water described as impure or hateful, and children having to manage with inadequate daily supply. In ELURU (Dwaraka Tirumala), one parent reported: "There is no proper water. Muddy water we are drinking." In SRIKAKULAM (Pathapatnam), children cannot bathe, wash clothes, or attend to daily hygiene due to supply failure. Parents in KURNOOL explicitly demand RO plant installation. This issue spans rural Gurukulams and affects hundreds of students in residential settings.

**Affected Districts:**  
NANDYAL, Y.S.R., KURNOOL, SPSR NELLORE, ANANTAPUR, ELURU, TIRUPATI, PRAKASAM, SRIKAKULAM, ANNAMAYYA, EAST GODAVARI, VISAKHAPATANAM, PALNADU, KRISHNA, BAPATLA, WEST GODAVARI, CHITTOOR

**Affected Schools:**  
NANDYAL district: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Gurukulam LAXMAPURAM (8 complaints), Gurukulam ALLAGADDA (RPRP), Gurukulam KOILAKUNTLA, Gurukulam PEAPALLY; Y.S.R. district: Gurukulam CHINNACHOWK (7 complaints), Gurukulam KAMALAPURAM (5 complaints), Gurukulam MADAKALAVARIPALLI; KURNOOL: Gurukulam KURNOOL, Gurukulam ADHONI, Gurukulam VELDURTHY; SPSR NELLORE: Gurukulam BUTCHIREDDIPALEM (4 complaints), APTWRS KODAVALURU; ANANTAPUR: Gurukulam THIMMAPUR, Gurukulam GOOTY; ELURU: Gurukulam DWARAKA TIRUMALA (3 complaints); SRIKAKULAM: Gurukulam PATHAPATNAM (3 complaints); plus 15+ additional schools across remaining districts.

**Risk Level:** Critical

**Suggested Action:**  
The District Education Officers (DEOs) of NANDYAL, Y.S.R., KURNOOL, and ELURU must initiate an emergency physical inspection of water infrastructure at all flagged schools within 15 working days. A mandatory RO plant installation or servicing audit should be commissioned for all residential Gurukulams. Schools supplying bore water directly to children must be issued compliance notices, and filtered/purified water availability must be verified as a minimum standard. A state-level dashboard tracking water facility status per school should be created and updated quarterly.

**Challenge Title:** Complete Absence of Sports and Extracurricular Activities

**Description:**  
Parents across 13 districts report that children in residential Gurukulams have no access to sports, physical education, or extracurricular activities. The primary cause is absent or non-functional PET (Physical Education Teacher) positions. In NANDYAL (Peapally), a parent observed: "From class 6 to 8, we have never seen children playing sports. They have not participated in any competition." In DR.B.R.AMBEDKAR KONASEEMA (Razole): "No activities, no participated games and sports." Parents in PALNADU note a single PET cannot serve the entire school. Schools in ANANTAPUR (Korrapadu), ELURU (Dwaraka Tirumala), SPSR NELLORE, WEST GODAVARI, and CHITTOOR report zero provision of sports. The situation is compounded by a lack of playgrounds in schools like KURNOOL (APTWRS Aluru).

**Affected Districts:**  
ANANTAPUR, SPSR NELLORE, DR.B.R.AMBEDKAR KONASEEMA, NANDYAL, EAST GODAVARI, ELURU, TIRUPATI, PRAKASAM, PALNADU, KURNOOL, BAPATLA, CHITTOOR, WEST GODAVARI

**Affected Schools:**  
Gurukulam KORRAPADU (3 complaints), Gurukulam PEAPALLY, APTWRJC P.KONAVALASA, Gurukulam RAZOLE (BCRS), APTWRS KODAVALURU, Gurukulam DWARAKA TIRUMALA, Gurukulam BAPATLA, APTWRS ALURU, Gurukulam SINGARAYAKONDA, Gurukulam SRIKALAHASTI, Gurukulam CHIMAKURTHY — and 11+ additional schools.

**Risk Level:** High

**Suggested Action:**  
The Sports and Physical Education wing under the AP Education Department must conduct a district-by-district audit of PET vacancies in all Gurukulam schools. Schools where PET positions have been vacant for more than 60 days must be issued immediate recruitment notices. Where permanent appointments are pending, contracted PET facilitators should be engaged. All Gurukulam campuses must meet minimum playground area standards as per existing CBSE/state norms. Parents must be notified of a monthly sports schedule through the PTM communication channel.

**Challenge Title:** Poor and Unsafe Food Quality in Residential Schools

**Description:**  
Parents across 12 districts reported serious concerns about the quality of food served in Gurukulam hostels. The most alarming report came from NTR district (Muppalla): "sometimes in rice they see some insects and other problems." In ANANTAPUR (Gooty), a parent rated food 1 stating "very bad food." In Y.S.R. (Kondapuram): "upma is not perfect, night time menu is also not perfect, all food items are fully spicy." Uncooked rice was reported in KURNOOL (Pattikonda). In ELURU (Dwaraka Tirumala), "no proper cooking" was reported. One parent in KURNOOL (Chinnatekuru) noted food was excellent in first year but "very poor" in second year, suggesting contractor changes or reduced oversight. Menu non-adherence was reported in PRAKASAM (Cumbum).

**Affected Districts:**  
NTR, ANANTAPUR, Y.S.R., DR.B.R.AMBEDKAR KONASEEMA, KURNOOL, NANDYAL, TIRUPATI, ELURU, PRAKASAM, ANNAMAYYA, BAPATLA, SRIKAKULAM

**Affected Schools:**  
Gurukulam School Muppalla, Gurukulam GOOTY (DPIP), Gurukulam KONDAPURAM, Gurukulam RAZOLE (BCRS), Gurukulam CHINNATEKURU (JR COL), Gurukulam PATTIKONDA, Gurukulam DWARAKA TIRUMALA, Gurukulam PEAPALLY, Gurukulam THIMMAPUR, Gurukulam CUMBUM, Gurukulam SINGARAYAKONDA, Gurukulam VELDURTHY

**Risk Level:** Critical

**Suggested Action:**  
A mandatory unannounced food quality inspection must be conducted at all flagged schools by the District Social Welfare Officer and the Gurukulam food contractor supervisor within 30 days. Insect or pest contamination in food is a public health violation and warrants immediate replacement of the kitchen contractor at Muppalla. District-level nutrition monitors must verify menu adherence monthly by comparing served meals against the approved diet chart. An anonymous child feedback mechanism for food quality should be introduced at the PTM level.

**Challenge Title:** Absent or Abusive Caretakers in Residential Hostels

**Description:**  
Caretaker-related complaints appear across 9 districts. Two distinct failure modes are observed: (1) positions are vacant or temporary, and (2) caretakers who are present exhibit rude or abusive behaviour toward children. In KURNOOL (Chinnatekuru), a parent explicitly stated: "caretaker was not good, he always scolds students, the way he talks was rash and rude — better to replace caretaker." In SRI SATHYA SAI (Hindupur), a parent reported the caretaker "chala sarlu thittaru" (repeatedly scolds students). In ANANTAPUR (Thimmapur), a teacher is informally performing caretaker duties, described as "detrimental to children's education." ELURU (Dwaraka Tirumala) had 4 caretaker-related complaints — the most of any school. In ANANTAPUR (Kurugunta), parents explicitly rated caretaker 2 "due to neglect of children."

**Affected Districts:**  
TIRUPATI, KURNOOL, ELURU, ANANTAPUR, SRI SATHYA SAI, EAST GODAVARI, PRAKASAM, KRISHNA, NANDYAL

**Affected Schools:**  
Gurukulam DWARAKA TIRUMALA (4 complaints), Gurukulam CHINNATEKURU (JR COL) (3 complaints), Gurukulam DAKKILI (3 complaints), Gurukulam KURUGUNTA (2 complaints), Gurukulam THIMMAPUR (2 complaints), Gurukulam HINDUPUR (DPIP) (2 complaints), Gurukulam CHIMAKURTHY (2 complaints), Gurukulam ADHONI, Gurukulam RUDRAVARAM, Gurukulam KONDEPI, Gurukulam ALLAGADDA (RPRP)

**Risk Level:** High

**Suggested Action:**  
The Social Welfare Department must audit caretaker rosters at all residential Gurukulams to confirm that designated, permanent caretakers are in position — not substitute teachers. Schools in ELURU, KURNOOL, and TIRUPATI with specific rude-behaviour complaints should be referred to the district-level grievance cell for immediate investigation. A formal code of conduct for caretakers must be enforced, with a parent-accessible complaint number displayed in all hostels. Child protection officers must be briefed on schools with repeated caretaker complaints.

**Challenge Title:** Teacher Shortage and Absence of Parent Communication

**Description:**  
Two intersecting problems emerge from teacher communication feedback. First, teacher vacancies are creating academic disruption. In TIRUPATI (Dakkili), a parent stated: "Teachers are not working and staff are very few. For some subjects there are no teachers at all." In KURNOOL (Chinnatekuru), lecturers were absent for four full months after the college reopened. Second, parents are excluded from information about their child's progress. In NANDYAL (Allagadda), a parent reported: "We don't know if there is any progress in the children. The teachers have not even called to say this is the situation of your child." In ANANTAPUR (Thimmapur), parents report receiving no information at all. In KURNOOL (C Belgal), "They are not giving information" about the child.

**Affected Districts:**  
TIRUPATI, KURNOOL, NANDYAL, ANANTAPUR, PRAKASAM, CHITTOOR, PARVATHIPURAM MANYAM

**Affected Schools:**  
Gurukulam DAKKILI (teacher absence + communication failure), Gurukulam CHINNATEKURU (JR COL) (4-month lecturer vacancy), Gurukulam ALLAGADDA (RPRP) (no parent communication), Gurukulam THIMMAPUR (no information sharing), Gurukulam KURUGUNTA (no subject teacher interaction), Gurukulam KUPPAM (DPIP), APTWRJC P.KONAVALASA (unqualified teachers), Gurukulam KONDEPI (teachers using bad words), Gurukulam C BELGAL

**Risk Level:** High

**Suggested Action:**  
The DEO must immediately verify teaching staff deployment registers at DAKKILI and CHINNATEKURU schools. Any school with subject-level teacher vacancies beyond 30 days must invoke the emergency guest lecturer empanelment scheme. A mandatory parent SMS or WhatsApp update on child progress must be issued at least once per term from each school. PTM attendance by teachers should be verified against records, and schools without at least bi-monthly parent communication must be flagged in district reviews.

(Kondapuram): "my daughter is suffering from throat pain and hiccups — there is no person understanding their problems." At Y.S.R. (Chinnachowk): "Worst. Not caring for students in any kind of situation, only blaming the students." In NANDYAL (Koilakuntla), two separate parents reported that sanitary pads are not provided to girl students — a basic health entitlement. At KURNOOL (APTWRS Aluru): "No health care." The SPSR NELLORE (Sarvepalli) feedback (rating 3) reported the school nurse cannot answer properly and has anger management issues. In ELURU (Dwaraka Tirumala), "Government tablets are too bad" — indicating issues with the government-supplied medicine kit.

**Affected Districts:**
