# Qualitative Report: AP PTM School Survey Feedback Analysis

**Date:** April 24, 2026  
**Subject:** Qualitative Analysis of Parent Feedback and School Performance Red-Flags

---

## Executive Summary

This report provides a qualitative analysis of parent feedback collected through the AP PTM School Survey. Out of approximately 17,000 total responses, 11,408 were identified as meaningful, with 1,692 specific complaints and red-flags (ratings 1-3). The analysis highlights critical infrastructure gaps, personnel performance issues, and recurring systemic challenges across several districts, with **Anantapur**, **Nandyal**, and **Tirupati** showing the highest concentration of issues.

## 3. Challenges That Require Attention

### **A. Chronic Water Purification Infrastructure Failure**
- **Description:** Multiple schools report that while RO plants exist, they are frequently non-functional or provide "salt water."
- **Affected Districts:** Anantapur, Kurnool, Y.S.R, Tirupati.
- **Risk Level:** High (Health & Safety).
- **Suggested Action:** Immediate audit of RO systems and mandatory maintenance contracts for all Gurukulams.

### **B. Quality Control in Diet and Menu Adherence**
- **Description:** Significant dissatisfaction with the quality of rice and lack of breakfast variety. Reports of hygiene issues (insects) are a major red flag.
- **Affected Districts:** East Godavari, Anantapur, Tirupati.
- **Risk Level:** High (Nutrition & Hygiene).
- **Suggested Action:** Surprise inspections of food storage and kitchen preparation areas.

### **C. English Language Proficiency & Teaching Quality**
- **Description:** Parents expected better English communication skills development. There is a gap between "curriculum" and "actual speaking skills."
- **Affected Districts:** Annamayya, SPSR Nellore, Sri Sathya Sai.
- **Risk Level:** Medium (Academic Integrity).
- **Suggested Action:** Implement dedicated English speaking modules and teacher training workshops.
